This page walks you through the full LYF x AI workshop experience — from the moment you register to the follow-up resources you receive after the session wraps. Knowing what to expect means you can show up prepared, get the most out of every hour, and keep building momentum long after the workshop ends.

## Before the Workshop

A little preparation goes a long way. Follow these steps to make sure you're ready to hit the ground running on the day of your session.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register on the LYF x AI Website">
    Head to the LYF x AI website and sign up for your chosen workshop. You'll receive a confirmation email with the session date, time, and any specific details for that workshop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Pre-Workshop Setup">
    Most workshops require you to install tools or review short prep materials before the session starts. Check your confirmation email or the workshop's detail page for a setup checklist — completing this ahead of time means you won't lose workshop time to installation hiccups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the Workshop Communication Channel">
    You'll be invited to a dedicated Slack or Discord channel for your workshop cohort. Join it before the session to receive last-minute updates, connect with other attendees, and get any additional prep resources the instructor shares.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you run into any issues during setup, post in the communication channel — instructors and fellow attendees are usually quick to help.
</Tip>

## During the Workshop

Sessions are typically **2–3 hours** long and follow a consistent rhythm designed to maximize your hands-on time without skipping the foundational context.

Each workshop moves through five phases:

<Accordion title="Brief Introduction">
  The instructor opens with a quick overview of the session goals and agenda. You'll know exactly what you're building toward before any instruction begins.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Concept Explanation">
  Key ideas are introduced clearly and concisely. The focus is on the mental models you need to complete the exercises — not exhaustive theory.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Live Demo">
  The instructor demonstrates the tool or technique in real time, narrating their thinking as they go. You can follow along or simply watch — your choice.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Hands-On Exercise">
  This is the core of every workshop. You work through a structured exercise yourself, applying what you just saw. Instructors circulate (in live sessions) or provide guided checkpoints (in on-demand sessions) to keep you on track.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Q&A">
  Each section closes with open Q&A. Bring your edge cases, your specific use cases, and your "what if I tried…" questions — this is where the real learning often happens.
</Accordion>

<Warning>
  Live workshops are not recorded for general distribution, but registered attendees always receive a private recording link after the session. If you have to step away, you won't miss out permanently.
</Warning>

## After the Workshop

Your learning doesn't stop when the session ends. Every registered participant receives a follow-up package within 24 hours that includes:

- **Session recording** — rewatch the full workshop at your own pace
- **Resource links** — curated references, documentation, and further reading for every topic covered
- **Cheat sheets** — concise, printable references for commands, prompts, and workflows from the session
- **Community access** — the workshop channel stays open so you can ask follow-up questions, share what you build, and connect with your cohort

<Note>
  All workshop materials — cheat sheets, resource links, and reference guides — are published in the **Resources** section of this knowledge base so you can find them anytime, even between workshops.
</Note>
