LYF x AI workshops are interactive training sessions built for professionals and teams who want real, practical experience with AI tools — not just slides and theory. Every workshop is designed around doing: you'll work through live demos, guided exercises, and real-world scenarios that give you skills you can apply the moment you leave.

## Why Workshops?

Self-study has its place, but nothing accelerates learning like guided, hands-on practice with an expert in the room. Our workshops combine structured instruction, live tool demonstrations, and exercises you complete in real time — so you're not just watching AI work, you're using it yourself.

By working through challenges alongside instructors and peers, you build intuition that's hard to develop alone. You get immediate feedback, answers to your specific questions, and the confidence that comes from having actually done the thing — not just read about it.

<Info>
  Workshops are available both **live** (scheduled sessions with an instructor) and **on-demand** (self-paced recordings with full materials). Either way, you get the same hands-on exercises and resources.
</Info>

## Who Should Attend?

Our workshops are designed for anyone who wants to use AI tools effectively in their work — no advanced technical background required. You'll get the most out of a LYF x AI workshop if you are:

- A **developer or engineer** who wants to move faster with AI-assisted coding and tooling
- A **product manager or designer** looking to prototype, research, and iterate with AI support
- An **entrepreneur or founder** exploring how AI can give your team an outsized advantage
- A **team lead or manager** building an AI-ready culture and workflow for your organization
- Simply **curious about AI tools** in practice and ready to move beyond surface-level experimentation

<Note>
  Some workshops (like Claude Code) involve installing tools on your machine beforehand. Check the individual workshop page for any prerequisites — most are beginner-friendly.
</Note>
