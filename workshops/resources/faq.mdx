Find quick answers to common questions about LYF x AI workshops. If your question isn't covered here, reach out via the [Contact](/about/contact) page and we'll get back to you promptly.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I register for a workshop?" icon="ticket">
    Visit the [LYF x AI website](https://lyfxai.com) and navigate to the **Workshops** page. Select the session you'd like to attend and complete the registration form. You'll receive a confirmation email shortly after with full details — including the session link or venue address, any preparation steps, and what to bring.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are workshops available online?" icon="globe">
    Yes. All LYF x AI workshops are available live online, and select sessions are also offered in-person. After each live session, a recording is shared with all registered participants so you can revisit the material or catch up if something comes up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I need to bring or prepare?" icon="laptop">
    Bring a laptop with the software listed in the [Setup Guide](/workshops/claude-code/setup) installed before the session starts. Beyond that, bring your curiosity — no prior AI experience is needed. The more questions you come in with, the more you'll get out of the workshop.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the Claude Code workshop suitable for beginners?" icon="seedling">
    Yes. The Claude Code workshop is designed to start from scratch and build up progressively. Basic familiarity with a terminal (opening it, running a command) is helpful but not required — the setup guide walks you through everything step by step.

    <Tip>
      If you've never used a terminal before, spend 10–15 minutes on a beginner terminal tutorial before the session. It'll make the exercises feel much more comfortable.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long are the workshops?" icon="clock">
    Most workshops run for **2–3 hours**, including hands-on exercises and a live Q&A at the end. The schedule for each session is included in your confirmation email. Plan to stay for the full duration — the Q&A is often where the most useful insights come up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I have access to the materials after the workshop?" icon="folder-open">
    Yes. All slides, cheat sheets, and exercise files from every workshop are published here in this knowledge base. You can access them at any time — during the workshop, immediately after, or weeks later when you want to revisit something.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I request a private workshop for my team?" icon="users">
    Absolutely. LYF x AI offers private and corporate workshop sessions tailored to your team's goals, stack, and experience level. Head to the [Contact](/about/contact) page to start the conversation and we'll put together a proposal for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I miss a session or fall behind during a live workshop?" icon="rotate-left">
    Don't worry — recordings of live sessions are made available to all registered participants after the event. Written guides, cheat sheets, and exercise walkthroughs are also published in this knowledge base, so you can work through everything at your own pace and come back to anything you need more time on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a paid Anthropic account?" icon="credit-card">
    No. A free Anthropic API key is sufficient to complete all workshop exercises. The free tier gives you enough usage to work through every hands-on task. Full details on obtaining and configuring your API key are in the [Setup Guide](/workshops/claude-code/setup).

    <Note>
      If you plan to continue using Claude Code heavily after the workshop, a paid plan offers higher rate limits. But for the workshop itself, the free tier is all you need.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
