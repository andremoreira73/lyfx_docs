LYF x AI takes a pragmatic approach to AI. Everything we build and teach starts with a real problem and ends with a practical solution. We don't design for the demo — we design for the day after the demo, when you're back at your desk and need things to actually work.

## How We Build Apps

Our apps are designed around specific user goals, with minimal friction standing between you and results. We ask one question before building anything: *what does the person using this actually need to accomplish?*

AI does the heavy lifting — processing, reasoning, generating — while you stay in control of the decisions that matter. We deliberately avoid complexity for its own sake. If a feature doesn't directly serve your goal, it doesn't ship. The result is software that feels natural to use, even when sophisticated AI is running under the hood.

We also build with real constraints in mind: time pressure, varying technical backgrounds, and the reality that most people are fitting new tools into already-busy workflows. That shapes everything from onboarding flows to how we surface AI outputs.

## How We Design Workshops

Every LYF x AI workshop follows a deliberate structure that maximizes what you retain and can actually apply.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with why">
    Every workshop begins with the problem AI can solve — not the technology itself. You understand the *why* before you ever touch the tool. This grounds everything that follows in genuine relevance to your work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show, don't just tell">
    Live demonstrations come before exercises so you see real behavior in real conditions. You watch how the tool responds, where it surprises, and how an experienced practitioner navigates it — before you're asked to do it yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Practice immediately">
    Hands-on exercises follow every concept explanation. You apply what you just saw within minutes, while it's fresh. Practice happens in realistic scenarios, not toy examples — so the learning transfers directly to your actual work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reflect and apply">
    Each session ends with facilitated discussion: How will you use this in your own work? What would you adapt? What questions remain? Reflection turns a workshop experience into a lasting shift in how you work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Makes Us Different

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Practical Focus">
    We prioritize real-world applicability over theoretical knowledge. Every exercise uses realistic scenarios drawn from the kinds of work our attendees actually do — not contrived examples designed to make the AI look good.

    When a tool has rough edges or limitations, we show those too. You leave knowing how to work with AI effectively, including when to push back, course-correct, or take a different approach entirely.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Expert Facilitation">
    Our workshop facilitators are active practitioners who use these tools daily. When you ask a question, you get an answer grounded in real experience — not a rehearsed script.

    Facilitators bring current knowledge into every session, including recent updates, new techniques, and honest assessments of what's changed. You're learning from people who are genuinely invested in these tools and in your success with them.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Ongoing Support">
    Learning doesn't stop when the workshop ends. Resources, session recordings, reference materials, and community access stay available to you after every LYF x AI workshop.

    We believe the real value of a workshop often shows up days or weeks later, when you're applying something you learned to a new situation. We make sure you have what you need when that moment comes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
