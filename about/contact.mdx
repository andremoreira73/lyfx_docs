## Workshop & Training Inquiries

If you're interested in bringing a LYF x AI workshop to your organization — whether that's a private session on Claude Code, a broader AI tools training for your team, or a speaking engagement — we'd love to talk.

Send us an email with a brief description of your team, your goals, and any timing or format preferences. The more context you share, the better we can tailor a proposal for you.

For corporate training engagements, include the approximate team size and whether you're looking for a one-time workshop or an ongoing series. We work with teams of all sizes and can adapt our content to different technical backgrounds.

## App Support

If you have a question or run into an issue with any LYF x AI app, we're here to help. Before reaching out, it's worth checking the relevant FAQ section in this knowledge base — most common questions are answered there, and it's often the fastest path to a solution.

If you've checked the docs and still need help, email us at [hello@lyf-x-ai.com](mailto:hello@lyf-x-ai.com) with:
- The name of the app you're using
- A brief description of the issue or question
- Any error messages or screenshots that might help us understand what you're seeing

We aim to respond to all support requests within 24–48 hours on business days.
