In this guide, you'll go from zero to running your first task with a LYF x AI app. By the end, you'll have accessed the platform, chosen an app that fits your needs, configured your preferences, and seen real output — all in just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the App">
    Visit the [LYF x AI platform](https://lyfxai.com) and log in with your account. If you're new, create a free account in under a minute — no credit card required to get started.

    Once you're logged in, navigate to the **App Catalog** from the main dashboard. This is your central hub for all available LYF x AI apps, organized by category and use case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your App">
    Browse the catalog and select the app that best fits what you want to accomplish. Each app includes a short description, a list of example use cases, and a preview of the kind of output it produces.

    Not sure where to start? Look for the **Recommended** badge on apps that are popular with users who share your role or industry. You can also use the search bar to find apps by keyword.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Settings">
    Before running your first task, take a moment to set your preferences. Depending on the app, you may be able to configure options such as output language, tone, format, or length.

    Don't worry about getting everything perfect upfront — the defaults are carefully chosen to work well for most users. You can always adjust your settings between runs to fine-tune your results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run Your First Task">
    Enter your input in the provided field — this might be a prompt, a piece of text, a file upload, or a set of parameters, depending on the app. When you're ready, click **Run** to let the AI get to work.

    Review the output and see how it fits your needs. If you want to refine the result, tweak your input or settings and run it again. Iteration is part of the process, and most users find their best results after just one or two adjustments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Once you've run your first task, head over to the [Features](/apps/features) page to discover the full range of capabilities built into LYF x AI apps — you might find tools and shortcuts that make your workflow even faster.
</Tip>
