Got a question about LYF x AI apps? You're in the right place. This FAQ covers the most common questions we hear from users — from getting started and understanding what the apps can do, to troubleshooting outputs and reaching our team when you need extra help.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I access the LYF x AI apps?">
    Accessing LYF x AI apps is straightforward:

    1. Visit the [LYF x AI website](https://lyfxai.com).
    2. Click **Sign In** in the top navigation if you already have an account, or select **Get Started** to create a free account — it only takes a minute.
    3. Once signed in, navigate to the **Apps** section to see all available tools and launch the one you need.

    No installation is required. All apps run directly in your browser on any modern device.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need any technical knowledge to use the apps?">
    Not at all. LYF x AI apps are designed for users at every skill level — from complete beginners to seasoned professionals. If you can type a sentence, you can use the apps.

    The interfaces are clean and intuitive, with in-app guidance, tooltips, and sensible defaults that make it easy to get good results from your very first session. If you want to go deeper, advanced settings are available but never required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What types of tasks can the apps help with?">
    LYF x AI apps are versatile tools that can assist with a wide range of tasks, including:

    - **Writing & editing** — drafts, summaries, rewrites, proofreading, and tone adjustments
    - **Brainstorming** — generating ideas, exploring angles, and expanding on concepts
    - **Analysis** — breaking down complex information, extracting key points, and structuring insights
    - **Automation** — handling repetitive text tasks at scale with batch processing
    - **Communication** — emails, messages, reports, and presentations tailored to your audience
    - **Content creation** — blog posts, social media copy, product descriptions, and more

    If your task involves working with language or information, there's a good chance a LYF x AI app can help.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I improve the quality of outputs?">
    Output quality is directly linked to the clarity and detail of your input. Here are the most effective ways to get better results:

    - **Write clear, specific prompts** — describe exactly what you want, who it's for, and what format you need.
    - **Use Custom Instructions** — set your preferred tone, style, language, and length before generating.
    - **Provide examples** — if you have a reference piece that matches the style you're after, include it in your prompt.
    - **Iterate** — treat the first output as a starting point. Refine your prompt based on what's close but not perfect, and regenerate.

    For a full guide on prompt-writing best practices, see the [How to Use](/apps/how-to-use) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use the apps on mobile?">
    Yes. All LYF x AI apps use a fully responsive, mobile-friendly interface that works seamlessly on smartphones and tablets. Open your browser, sign in to your account, and use the apps just as you would on a desktop — no separate app download needed.

    For the best experience on smaller screens, we recommend using your device in portrait orientation and enabling your browser's reader mode if you find the layout too dense.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data private?">
    Your privacy matters to us. LYF x AI handles your data with care:

    - Your inputs and outputs are processed securely and are **not shared with third parties**.
    - We do not sell your data or use it for advertising purposes.
    - Data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit.
    - You can delete your account and associated data at any time from your account settings.

    For full details, refer to our Privacy Policy linked in the footer of every page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I do if an output seems incorrect?">
    AI-generated outputs are powerful but not infallible. If a result doesn't look right, here's what to do:

    1. **Refine your prompt** — add more context, be more specific, or correct any ambiguous phrasing that may have confused the AI.
    2. **Adjust Custom Instructions** — if the tone, format, or length is off, update your instructions and regenerate.
    3. **Use the feedback option** — click the thumbs-down or feedback icon on any output to flag an issue. This helps us improve the models over time.
    4. **Try rephrasing** — sometimes a slightly different way of asking the same question produces a much better result.

    If the issue persists or seems like a technical problem rather than a prompt issue, reach out to our support team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Our team is happy to help. For questions, feedback, or technical issues, visit the [Contact](/about/contact) page and send us a message directly. We aim to respond to all enquiries within one business day.

    You can also browse the rest of this documentation for answers — the [How to Use](/apps/how-to-use) and [Features](/apps/features) pages cover most common scenarios in detail.
  </Accordion>
