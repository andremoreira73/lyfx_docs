Whether you're using a LYF x AI app for the first time or looking to unlock more advanced workflows, this page has you covered. Find hands-on guides for the most common tasks below — pick the workflow that matches where you are right now, and follow along at your own pace.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Basic Usage">
    Get up and running quickly with this beginner-friendly walkthrough. You'll go from a blank screen to a finished, exportable output in just a few steps.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the app">
        Navigate to the LYF x AI app you want to use from the [Apps](/apps) section. Sign in to your account if prompted — or create a free account in under a minute.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your prompt or input">
        Type your request into the main input field. Be as specific as you like: describe the task, the audience, the tone, and any key details you want included. The more context you give, the better the result.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review the output">
        Once the AI generates a response, read through it carefully. Check that it meets your goals and matches the format you need. Most outputs are ready to use immediately or need only minor edits.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Refine if needed">
        Not quite right? Update your prompt with clearer instructions, add more context, or use the inline refinement tools to adjust tone, length, or style. You can iterate as many times as you like.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Export or copy the result">
        When you're satisfied, copy the output to your clipboard or download it in your preferred format. Use the export options to save as a file or share a link with others.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Advanced Usage">
    Ready to go deeper? These advanced workflows help you unlock the full power of LYF x AI apps — from custom instructions to chaining tasks and saving reusable templates.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Set custom instructions">
        Before running your prompt, open the **Custom Instructions** panel. Define your preferred tone, output format, language, length, and any specific rules the AI should follow. These instructions are applied to every generation in the session.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use batch input">
        Switch to **Batch Mode** in the app settings. Paste a list of inputs, upload a file with multiple items, or define a repeating pattern. The app processes all entries simultaneously and returns a consolidated set of outputs.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Chain tasks together">
        Use the output of one generation as the input for the next. For example, generate a list of ideas, then feed that list back in to expand each item into a full paragraph — building complex, multi-stage workflows without leaving the app.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save and reuse templates">
        Once you've dialled in a prompt and set of instructions that works well, save it as a **Template**. Templates store your prompt structure, custom instructions, and output preferences so you can re-run the same workflow instantly — great for recurring tasks or team-wide consistency.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Tips & Tricks">
    Get more out of every session with these power-user tips. Expand each item to learn the technique.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="How do I get better results?">
        The quality of your output is closely tied to the clarity of your prompt. Follow these guidelines for consistently strong results:

        - **Be specific**: Instead of "write me a summary," try "write a three-sentence summary of the following article for a general audience."
        - **Provide context**: Tell the AI who the content is for, what it will be used for, and any constraints (word count, tone, format).
        - **Use examples**: Paste in a sample of the style or structure you're aiming for and ask the AI to match it.
        - **Iterate**: Treat the first output as a draft. Refine your prompt based on what's close but not quite right, rather than starting from scratch.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Can I save my settings?">
        Yes — LYF x AI apps support **Templates** and **Saved Preferences** so you don't have to reconfigure your setup every session.

        - **Templates** store your full prompt structure, custom instructions, and output format settings. Create one by clicking **Save as Template** after configuring a workflow you're happy with.
        - **Saved Preferences** let you set default values for tone, language, and output format that apply automatically whenever you open the app.

        Access all your saved templates and preferences from your account dashboard under **My Templates**.
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="How do I process multiple items?">
        Use **Batch Mode** to handle multiple inputs in a single run — no need to submit them one at a time.

        1. Open the app and navigate to **Settings → Batch Mode**.
        2. Choose your input method: paste a list directly, upload a `.txt` or `.csv` file, or enter items line by line.
        3. Apply any custom instructions you want to apply across all items.
        4. Click **Run Batch** — the app processes every item and presents the outputs together.
        5. Review, edit individual results if needed, then export the full batch as a single file or copy items individually.

        Batch Mode is ideal for tasks like generating descriptions for a product catalogue, summarising multiple articles, or formatting a large dataset.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
