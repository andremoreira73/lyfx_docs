## Customization & Control

LYF x AI apps give you precise control over how your outputs look and feel. You're never locked into a one-size-fits-all result — every generation can be shaped to match your exact needs.

Adjust the **tone** of your content to be formal, casual, persuasive, or neutral. Control the **style** to suit different audiences, from technical readers to everyday consumers. Set a target **length** — from a single sentence to a full-length document — and the AI will scale its response accordingly. Switch the **language** to reach global audiences, with support for multiple languages across all major apps. Finally, choose your preferred **output format**: plain text, markdown, bullet points, structured lists, or formatted documents, depending on what your workflow demands.

These settings can be applied on a per-session basis or saved as a template so your preferences are automatically applied every time you open the app.

## Export & Integration

Once you're happy with your output, getting it out of the app and into your workflow is effortless. LYF x AI apps support a range of export and copy options designed to fit the tools you already use.

- **Copy to clipboard** — instantly paste your result into any document, email, or app with a single click.
- **Download as a file** — save your output as a `.txt`, `.md`, or `.docx` file directly to your device.
- **Export to common formats** — export structured content to formats compatible with popular productivity and publishing tools.
- **Share a link** — generate a shareable link to your output for easy collaboration with teammates or clients.

All exports retain the formatting and structure chosen during generation, so your output arrives exactly as intended.
